# Differentiation

PROOFCOURT is deliberately not an escrow marketplace or a two-party dispute
court.

Internet Court adjudicates disagreements between counterparties and connects a
GenLayer jury to escrow. Collective Memory and Agent Work Order center the job
marketplace/payment lifecycle. PROOFCOURT has no funds, opposing parties, or
settlement path. It is an application-neutral evidence-attestation layer.

Its primitive is a portable, historical Proof-of-Work Receipt: a criterion-level
audit of one agent delivery against live public sources. Marketplaces, grants,
DAOs, autonomous workflows, or reputation systems can consume the same receipt
through `is_verified` without adopting PROOFCOURT's UI or an escrow design.

The distinction is therefore **verification before or without dispute**, with
live evidence retrieval and immutable attempt history—not dispute resolution.
